UI-Mate Browser Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

UI-Mate Browser (the “extension”) is the Chrome extension for the UI-Mate desktop app. It lets a task you start in UI-Mate open and operate web pages in your Chrome. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

Data the extension reads

Only for the tabs a task opened itself:

  • The page’s accessibility tree: element roles, names, states and visible text. Values typed into editable fields are not included, and protected fields such as password boxes appear only as [protected].
  • Screenshots of that tab when the task needs one, and a live preview shown to you in UI-Mate. A screenshot contains whatever is visible on the page.
  • The tab’s title and URL, and events such as navigation, you taking over, or the task stopping.

The extension does not read your other tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, cookies or saved passwords.

Where the data goes

  • The extension communicates only with the UI-Mate app on the same computer (address 127.0.0.1). It connects to no other server. So that you never have to paste a code, it also uses Chrome’s native messaging to ask a small helper that UI-Mate registers on your computer for UI-Mate’s local address and a one-time connection credential; no page content passes through it.
  • The UI-Mate app sends the page text and screenshots to the AI model service you configured in the app, to decide the next step. UI-Mate does not operate a model service and does not preset one: you choose the service and enter its key in the app. Those services handle the data under their own privacy policies.
  • The live preview is shown only to you in UI-Mate and is not sent to a model service.

Data stored on your computer

  • The extension stores its pairing with UI-Mate (a local port and an access token) in Chrome’s extension storage so it can reconnect after a restart, and keeps the list of tasks under control until Chrome closes. Clicking “Disconnect” in the extension popup or removing the extension deletes it.
  • The UI-Mate app keeps task records on your computer, including page text and screenshots read during a task and the requests sent to the model service, in ~/Library/Application Support/UI-Mate-v04. Deleting a task in UI-Mate deletes its records; deleting that folder deletes all of them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • We do not use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending, or any purpose unrelated to the features above.
  • Neither the extension nor the UI-Mate app sends any data to servers run by the UI-Mate developers.

Your control

  • A task operates only tabs it opened itself, grouped in a blue tab group named after the task.
  • Click one of the task’s tabs and the assistant pauses so you can take over. You can stop a task at any time from the extension popup or from UI-Mate. Stopping releases the debugger and keeps the pages open.
  • Closing Chrome’s debugging bar at the top of the page also ends control immediately.

Limited Use

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Ask a question or report a problem on GitHub: github.com/UI-Mate/ui-mate.github.io/issues

UI-Mate Browser 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

UI-Mate Browser（下称「本扩展」）是 UI-Mate 桌面应用的 Chrome 扩展。它让你在 UI-Mate 里发起的任务，在你的 Chrome 中打开网页并完成操作。本政策说明本扩展处理哪些数据、发往哪里。

本扩展读取的数据

只针对任务自己打开的标签页：

  • 页面的无障碍树：元素的类型、名称、状态和可见文字。可编辑输入框里的内容不包含在内，受保护的字段（如密码框）只显示为 [protected]。
  • 截图：任务需要时截取的该标签页画面，以及在 UI-Mate 里给你看的实时预览图。截图会包含页面上看得见的任何内容。
  • 标签页的标题和网址，以及页面跳转、你接管或停止任务等事件。

本扩展不读取你的其他标签页、浏览记录、书签、下载内容、Cookie 或已保存的密码。

数据发往哪里

  • 本扩展只和同一台电脑上的 UI-Mate 应用通信（地址 127.0.0.1），不连接任何其他服务器。为了不用粘贴连接码，它还会通过 Chrome 的 native messaging 向 UI-Mate 在本机注册的小程序询问 UI-Mate 的本机地址和一次性连接凭据；这条通道不传送任何网页内容。
  • UI-Mate 应用会把页面文字和截图发给你在应用里配置的 AI 模型服务，用来决定下一步操作。UI-Mate 不运营模型服务，也不预设任何服务：用哪家服务、用哪个密钥由你在应用里填写。这些服务按各自的隐私政策处理数据。
  • 实时预览图只在 UI-Mate 里显示给你看，不发给模型服务。

保存在本机的数据

  • 本扩展在 Chrome 的扩展存储里保存与 UI-Mate 的配对信息（本机端口和访问令牌），以便重启后自动重连；在 Chrome 关闭前保存当前受控任务的列表。在扩展弹窗点「Disconnect」或卸载本扩展即会删除。
  • UI-Mate 应用会在本机保存任务记录，包括任务过程中读到的页面文字和截图，以及发给模型服务的请求记录，位置是 ~/Library/Application Support/UI-Mate-v04。在 UI-Mate 里删除任务会删除它的记录；删除这个文件夹会删除全部记录。

我们不做的事

  • 不出售或出租数据。
  • 不把数据用于广告、用户画像、信用评估或借贷，也不用于与上述功能无关的任何目的。
  • 本扩展和 UI-Mate 应用都不向 UI-Mate 开发者的服务器发送任何数据。

你的控制权

  • 任务只操作它自己打开的标签页，并放在一个以任务命名的蓝色标签页分组里。
  • 点一下任务的标签页，助手就会暂停，由你接手；你可以随时在扩展弹窗或 UI-Mate 里停止任务。停止后本扩展释放调试连接，网页保留。
  • 关闭 Chrome 页面上方的调试提示条也会立即结束控制。

Limited Use 声明

本扩展对所获取信息的使用遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其中的 Limited Use（有限使用）要求。
